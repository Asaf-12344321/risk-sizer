Your rules
Everything here is typed in by you from the Yahoo Finance app — Beta and Avg Volume on the Statistics/Summary tab, ATR(14) as a Chart indicator, support/resistance and 52-week range visible on the chart, earnings date on the Summary tab. This tool has no live market connection. Position size is bound by the tightest of beta rule, capital cap, and liquidity cap. The stop uses ATR floored/capped by nearby support — beta doesn't capture stock-specific noise, which is what a stop actually needs to survive. Treat every number here as a starting point, not gospel — especially the 52-week-drawdown and earnings-window flags, which need your judgment, not just math.
