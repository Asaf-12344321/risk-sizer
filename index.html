Your rules

Position size = the smallest of: risk-based size, thesis-break cap, capital cap, beta-exposure cap, liquidity cap.

Size comes from the stop, not from beta: risking your budget over the ATR/support stop distance. That controls an ordinary loss. The thesis-break cap controls the other kind — a gap through the stop on news, dilution, or a story that dies — by asking what a bad gap costs at that size and holding it to your break budget. Beta is demoted to an exposure cap, so a high-beta name can't take a full slot. Everything is typed in by you from the Yahoo Finance app: ATR(14) as a chart indicator, Beta and Avg Volume on the Statistics tab, support/resistance and the 52-week range off the chart, earnings on Summary. No live market connection. Break-even win rate is the odds floor — below it the trade is negative-EV no matter how you size it.
