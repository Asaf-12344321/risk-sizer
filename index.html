Risk settings

Size = 1R ÷ stop%, then held down by the smallest cap that applies. The crash percentages come from measured 45-day drawdowns and are picked automatically from the stock's ATR.

Everything is sized from the stop: risk 1R, divide by the stop distance, then take the smallest cap. The stop starts at —× ATR and only ever moves up — once the price is — ahead it sits at your entry, which protects the trade 95% of the time. The other 5% is an overnight gap opening below the stop and filling there, and the tracker prices that fill at the open. Bars are daily OHLC from Yahoo, not live quotes.
